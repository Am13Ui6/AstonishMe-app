About the AstonishMe Workout Methodology

AstonishMe utilizes a classic concurrent training split combining heavy compound barbell and dumbbell strength training followed by high-intensity functional conditioning (metabolic conditioning).

Strength Prioritization

Heavy compound movements (Squats, Presses, Pulls) build bone density, tendon integrity, and neurological recruitment before high heart-rate work begins.

Conditioning Formats

Conditioning utilizes standard HIIT formats: AMRAP (As Many Rounds As Possible), For Time, EMOM (Every Minute on the Minute), and 20s/10s Tabata intervals.
