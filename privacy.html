Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AstonishMe ("we," "our," or "us") handles user privacy for our mobile application (the "App") and our website (the "Website").

We believe in complete transparency and a privacy-first approach. Because our App and Website do not require user accounts or register personal physical details, your workout routine remains entirely your own.

1. No Personal Data Collection

A. No Account Registration

You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical attributes like height and weight) to use the App or Website.

B. Local-Only Functionality (No Data Uploads)

Our App provides structured workout plans, exercises, and routines. Currently, we do not collect, generate, or request custom workout preferences, health metrics, or fitness goals. All workouts are preset and run entirely locally on your device.

If you track workouts or timers within the App, this data is stored strictly on your local device's storage and is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.

C. Website Behavior

Our Website acts as an informational extension of the App. Just like the App, the Website does not collect, store, or process any personal fitness data or user input.

2. Third-Party Services & Automatically Collected Data

To keep our App and Website free to use, we partner with Google to serve advertisements and manage privacy regulations. These third-party SDKs automatically collect certain device-level identifiers:

Google AdMob (Advertisements)

We use AdMob to display ads inside the App. AdMob may collect and use your device’s unique Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and general device specs to display relevant ads or measure ad performance.

Official Policy: Google Privacy & Terms

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK)

We use Google's UMP SDK to present a consent dialog to users (especially in the European Economic Area, UK, and select US states). This helps you control whether AdMob can serve you personalized or non-personalized ads.

Official Policy: Google Privacy & Terms

Google Sites (Website Hosting)

Our Website is hosted on Google Sites. Google may collect standard, anonymous web traffic statistics (such as browser type and pages visited) to help us analyze overall Website performance. No personal user data is captured.

3. GDPR and CCPA Compliance (Your Rights)

Under regional laws like the GDPR (Europe) and CCPA (California), you have control over how your device data is used for advertising:

  • In-App Controls: You can view, change, or withdraw your consent regarding personalized ads and cookie-like tracking at any time via the consent settings screen inside the App (managed by the Google UMP SDK).
  • Data Requests: Because we do not collect or store any accounts, emails, or user-specific fitness metrics on our servers, we have no personal data of yours to access, transfer, or delete.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal info, accounts, or location data, our App and Website are safe for general audiences. We do not knowingly target or collect information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our App's features (for example, if we introduce web-based tools in the future). Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
