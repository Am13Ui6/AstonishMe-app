AstonishMe Web & Mobile

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AstonishMe ("we," "our," or "us") handles user privacy when you access and use our website and web application (the "Website" or "Web App"), as well as our companion mobile applications.

We believe in complete transparency and a privacy-first philosophy. Our Web App is designed to function client-side in your web browser without requiring accounts, personal profiles, or health metric uploads. Your workout routines and fitness activity remain entirely in your hands.

1. No Personal Data Collection

A. No Account Registration Required

You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal identification (such as your name, email address, phone number, or biometric data like weight and height) to access the workouts, timers, and tools on our Website.

B. Client-Side Browser Storage (No Cloud Uploads)

The AstonishMe Web App provides structured exercise routines, timers, and workout tracking that run locally in your web browser. Any progress tracking, completion history, or active timer states are saved directly on your browser via local web storage (such as localStorage or IndexedDB).

This information is kept exclusively on your current device and browser. We do not transmit, back up, or store your workout logs on any external servers or databases. Clearing your browser cache or site data will remove your saved records.

2. Cookies, Web Technologies & Third-Party Services

Like most websites, we utilize standard browser technologies and third-party tools to keep the service fast, secure, and free:

A. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential local storage to remember your client-side preferences (such as light/dark mode settings, sound effects, and workout progress). Third-party advertising and analytics partners may also place or read cookies in your browser to deliver advertisements and analyze anonymized site usage.

B. Google Advertising (AdSense / AdMob)

We display advertisements to support the free operation of the Web App. Google and its ad partners may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites on the internet.

Opt-Out & Policy: You can learn more or opt out of personalized ad targeting by visiting Google Ad Settings and Google Privacy & Terms.

C. Cookie Consent & Management (CMP / UMP)

Where required by law (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and certain US states), we use a Consent Management Platform to provide you with granular control over cookies and personalized ad targeting upon visiting the Website.

D. Web Hosting & Server Logs

Our Website is hosted on web infrastructure that automatically records standard server access logs. These logs may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and access timestamps. These logs are used purely for cybersecurity, performance monitoring, and traffic analysis, and are never connected to any personal identity.

3. GDPR, CCPA, and Cookie Controls (Your Rights)

Under regional regulations like GDPR (Europe) and CCPA/CPRA (California), you have specific rights regarding your personal data and digital privacy:

  • Browser Cookie Controls: You can configure your web browser at any time to block or delete cookies, prevent third-party tracking, or clear local storage. Note that clearing local storage will reset your saved workout records on the Web App.
  • Consent Preferences: You can revisit and adjust your cookie and advertising consent preferences at any time using the cookie settings link available in the Website footer.
  • Data Access & Deletion: Because we do not operate a user database or store personal identifiers, we hold no personal profile data for you on our backend servers.

4. Children’s Privacy

The AstonishMe Web App does not collect personal contact information, live geolocation, or user profiles. It is safe for general audiences, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, updated web APIs, or changes in regulatory requirements. When updates are published, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be revised accordingly.
